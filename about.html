HOW IT WORKS

Our Calculation
Methodology

01

BMR Calculation

We use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation as the primary BMR formula, or the more accurate Katch-McArdle formula when body fat % is provided.

02

TDEE Adjustment

BMR is multiplied by an activity multiplier (1.2–1.9) based on validated research from Harris-Benedict adaptations.

03

Goal-Based Surplus/Deficit

A ~500 kcal deficit ≈ ~0.5 kg fat loss/week. We recommend conservative adjustments to preserve muscle mass.

04

Macro Distribution

Protein targets based on Helms et al. (2014). Carb/fat splits follow ISSN position stand guidelines.

GOT QUESTIONS

Common Questions

How accurate is the calorie calculator? +
Our calculator is within ±200 kcal for most people using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. Providing body fat % significantly improves accuracy via the Katch-McArdle formula.
Should I bulk or cut first? +
If your body fat is above 20% (men) or 28% (women), prioritize cutting first. If you're lean (under 15%/23%), bulk. If you're in between, a recomp or slight bulk is appropriate.
How long should a bulk or cut phase last? +
Bulks: 12–20 weeks. Cuts: 8–16 weeks depending on your goal. Avoid endless cuts — they tank testosterone and metabolism.
Is ForgeFit free to use? +
Yes, completely free. All calculators, diet plans, and tips are accessible to everyone.
